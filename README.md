## 🎯 What is DocuIntel?

DocuIntel is a friendly desktop program that reads your documents and answers questions about them. Think of it as a super-smart assistant that:

- Reads scanned papers and photos (OCR technology)
- Finds information across thousands of pages in seconds
- Shows you exactly where each answer came from
- Removes personal details (like phone numbers) automatically
- Works entirely offline – your documents never leave your PC

You don't need to be a tech expert. If you can click a button and type a question, you can use DocuIntel.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting (When Things Get Stuck)

### ❌ "Cannot connect to DocuIntel"
- Double-click the **DocuIntel** icon on your desktop
- Wait 10 seconds, then refresh your browser
- If nothing happens, restart your computer

### 📑 "My PDF won't read"
- Make sure it's not password-protected
- Try saving it again as "Latest PDF Format"
- For very old files, convert them to PDF again

### 💾 "Not enough memory"
- Close other heavy programs (games, video editors)
- Split your documents into smaller groups
- Restart DocuIntel

### 🖼️ "Photos are blurry"
- Retake photos with good lighting
- Hold steady and get closer to the text
- Use the "Enhance" button in DocuIntel
